# Vulcan learning illustrations

- Use real photographs to establish credible objects, gestures and contact points. Record the source, selection and any recomposition in the batch manifest.
- Treat photographs as evidence for function, not as product portraits. Ovens must be generic: use ordinary masonry openings or an unbranded domestic cavity, with no distinctive commercial silhouette, shell, folding feet, trim, vents or control layout. Removing a logo or changing a colour is insufficient.
- Preserve the mechanism being taught: the stone supports the pizza, the small turning peel enters under an edge, hands remain outside the oven, and a cleaning scene clearly represents the stated maintenance conditions. Simplifying the enclosure must not change these relationships.
- Keep the approved coloured pen style: terracotta and ivory, thin brown outlines, restrained smooth washes, transparent background. Avoid granular overlays, stippling, paper grain, exaggerated food texture and shiny plastic rendering.
- Put useful explanations and readings in localized HTML, not in the artwork. Keep photographic inspiration/recomposition details in the internal manifests; do not display them beside independently reinterpreted illustrations. Display credits for declared licensed adaptations/reused images, and retain scientific sources supporting the content. Never invent numeric thresholds or diagnose doneness from appearance alone.
- Retain previous images and original masters. Compare edited assets in the actual guide on desktop and mobile, and check the existing performance budget before publishing.

The generic-oven requirement applies to future batches as well as revisions of the current library. Reference tracking remains in the internal manifests even when the enclosure is generalized. Public attribution follows the distinction above, as requested by Matteo on 2026-11-01.
